# ADR-006: Evaluation constraints — TPC-C, YCSB, ransomware simulators

## Status
Accepted

## Context

Chronos must be evaluated under realistic concurrent storage workloads
and against active ransomware families, with quantitative latency and
overhead targets.

Constraints:
- Workloads: high-concurrency PostgreSQL TPC-C and RocksDB YCSB on NVMe
  storage.
- p99 I/O latency overhead must remain under 1.4% on benign traffic.
- Ransomware simulators: LockBit 3.0, BlackCat/ALPHV, Akira encryption
  routines.
- Neutralization target: 100%, zero committed byte loss.
- Average containment latency ~418 μs, broken down as:
  - ~82 μs eBPF interception
  - ~210 μs descriptor collection
  - ~126 μs extent restoration
- ~3,850× speedup over volume snapshot rollbacks (LVM/ZFS/btrfs).

Options Considered
1. **Real ransomware binaries** — highest fidelity but unsafe and hard to
   obtain; use dummy simulators executing known encryption routines
   instead.
2. **Synthetic random-write workload only** — safe but does not validate
   ransomware-specific entropy/magic-byte detection.
3. **Combined TPC-C + YCSB + dummy ransomware simulators** — validates
   both overhead and containment; chosen.

## Decision

Run PostgreSQL TPC-C and RocksDB YCSB on NVMe to measure p99 overhead,
and deploy dummy ransomware simulators executing LockBit 3.0,
BlackCat/ALPHV, and Akira encryption routines to measure containment and
rollback latency against the 418 μs / 82-210-126 μs targets.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Quantifies overhead on production-representative transactional traffic.
- Validates 100% neutralization and zero committed byte loss.
- Granular latency breakdown matches the documented targets.

### Negative
- Dummy simulators may not capture every variant behavior; results are
  indicative, not a guarantee against unseen families.
- NVMe-specific results may not generalize to slower storage.

## Revisit When

New ransomware families emerge or storage tier changes (e.g., SATA/SAS);
re-run the simulator matrix and update the latency targets.
